## Minor

### 🟡 The width check assumes the name is the widest line, `e2e/site.spec.ts:1546`
**Problem**: `within1px(block.width, await nameWidth(page))` asserts that `<main>` is exactly as wide as the `h1`'s text. That holds today because the name is 21 glyphs at 22px (277.2px on Mac, 273px in CI) and the tagline is 26 glyphs at 16px (249.6px or 260px). But `self-center` sizes the block to its widest line, whichever line that is, and the schema allows a tagline up to 27 glyphs (259.2px on Mac, 270px in CI) beside a name of any length. A name of about 20 characters or fewer with a tagline near the cap makes the tagline the widest line, and this assertion fails on valid content. The spec's Decision says "on today's content the name is widest", so the rule is known to be content dependent, while AC-10 promises that a valid content edit needs no test edit.
**Why it matters**: The site owner's name is unlikely to change, so this will not bite soon, but the test encodes a fact about today's words rather than the layout rule (`<main>` is as wide as its widest line). A shortened name or a longer tagline turns a passing layout into a red test, and the first instinct will be to loosen the assertion rather than fix content.
**Suggested fix**: Measure the widest line instead of the name: a `Range` around the `h1`'s contents, one around the tagline's, and one around each row link's contents (the prefix span to the label's right edge), then assert `block.width` is within 1px of the largest. The `nameWidth` helper already does the `Range` part; generalise it to take a locator. AC-4's wording ("equals the name's measured text width") can then say "its widest line's measured width", which is what the Decision describes.

### 🟡 The tagline cap is not tied to the column by a test, unlike the email cap, `e2e/site.spec.ts:1485`
**Problem**: `CV_LIMITS.tagline` is 27 because 27 glyphs at 10px fit the 272px phone column (`src/lib/cv-schema.ts:17`). Vitest proves 27 passes and 28 fails, and the page test at line 1478 proves today's 26 glyph tagline is one line tall at 320px. Nothing proves that 27 glyphs of the rendered font fit the rendered column. The contact block already closes this gap for the email cap: lines 2668 to 2674 multiply `CV_LIMITS.email` by the measured glyph width and assert the result fits the row. The tagline test has no such line.
**Why it matters**: The cap and the column are two numbers in two files. A change to the shell padding (`px-6` in `BaseLayout.astro:88`), the base font size, or the tagline's size or face leaves 27 wrong, and the first sign is a two line tagline on a phone after a content edit, which is the AC-7 promise broken silently.
**Suggested fix**: In the 320px tagline test, measure the tagline's text width with a `Range` (or the `<p>`'s box, since it is one line), divide by `basics.tagline.length` for the glyph advance, multiply by `CV_LIMITS.tagline`, and assert it is at most the column's content width (the wrapper's box width less the 48px of padding, not `<main>`'s width, which `self-center` can shrink below the column). It is one assertion in the same shape as the email one, and it fails the moment the cap, the font, or the column move out of step.

### 🟡 The scope says verified while the VoiceOver step stays open, `docs/scope/scope.md:185`
**Problem**: The scope ticks "Verify it" for the home page redesign, but `docs/specs/0008-home-page-centered-menu/verify.md:14` (VoiceOver reads the heading, the tagline, then "Pages, navigation" with four links and no numbers spoken) is still unticked. It is the only check that the `aria-hidden` numbers are silent in a real screen reader; axe cannot test that.
**Why it matters**: The scope is what the next `/scope` and `/sync` runs read. A ticked row with an open step behind it hides the one accessibility check that still needs a person. The same mismatch was flagged on the projects and contact page reviews. The stakes are lower here than on `/contact`, since the list sits inside a `<nav>` and Safari keeps its list role, but the record should match the work.
**Suggested fix**: Run the VoiceOver step on a Mac and tick it, or leave "Verify it" open in the scope with a note naming the step.
